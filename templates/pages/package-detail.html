About this package

The complete Himalayan pilgrimage

The Char Dham Yatra is the most sacred pilgrimage circuit in the Himalayas — Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath. Over ten unforgettable days you travel through the valleys of Garhwal with experienced local guides, comfortable hand-picked stays, and safe, well-maintained transport. We handle every detail so you can focus on the darshan and the mountains.

Package highlights

Darshan at all four dhams
Experienced local guides
Comfortable hand-picked stays
Safe, hill-experienced drivers
Women-friendly & family-safe

Day-by-day itinerary

{% comment %} each step {% endcomment %}
1

Haridwar → Barkot

Arrival, meet your guide, scenic drive to Barkot for an overnight stay.

2

Yamunotri Darshan

Trek/pony to Yamunotri temple, darshan, return to Barkot.

3

Barkot → Uttarkashi

Drive to Uttarkashi along the Bhagirathi; evening at leisure.

4

Gangotri Darshan

Visit Gangotri, source of the Ganga, and return to Uttarkashi.

5–7

Kedarnath Darshan

Drive to Guptkashi, then trek or helicopter to Kedarnath for darshan and stay.

8–9

Badrinath Darshan

Journey to Badrinath, darshan, Mana village visit, then to Rudraprayag.

10

Return to Haridwar

Scenic drive back to Haridwar with fond memories — trip concludes.

Frequently asked

When is the best time for Char Dham Yatra?

The yatra runs from May to October, when the temples are open. May–June and September are the most pleasant.

How fit do I need to be?

Basic fitness helps for the Kedarnath and Yamunotri treks. Pony, palki and helicopter options are available for those who prefer.

Is it women-friendly?

Absolutely. Safe stays, respectful guides and thoughtful arrangements make this comfortable for women and families.
