Our journey

Rooted in the mountains we love

Augy Travels was born in the valleys of Kedarnath, out of a simple belief: travel to the Himalayas should be safe, honest and unforgettable. From our head office in the Kedarnath valley — with branches in Delhi and Dehradun — a team of experienced travel operators works day and night to make every journey smooth and memorable.

We keep things clear and uncomplicated. No last-minute extras you didn't know about, no surprise charges — just straightforward service you can trust. Beyond the Char Dham, we craft religious and nature trips to Rishikesh, Haridwar, Mussoorie, Auli and Chopta, wildlife tours in Corbett, and restful stays in Nainital.

Above all, we're passionate about safe, women-friendly travel — every woman should feel secure venturing into the mountains with us, with comfort, safety and support always at the forefront.

Our Mission

To make Himalayan pilgrimage and mountain travel safe, affordable and effortless for every traveller — handling every detail so you can focus on the experience.

Our Vision

To be Uttarakhand's most trusted, women-friendly travel company — known for honesty, care and genuine local expertise across the Garhwal Himalayas.
