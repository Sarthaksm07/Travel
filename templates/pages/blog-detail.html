Kedarnath Heli Ticket Price: Booking Guide & Cost Breakdown

A
By Admin
·Jun 11, 2026 ·8 min read

Planning a Kedarnath darshan and short on time? The helicopter service is the fastest, most comfortable way to reach the temple — especially for elders and families. Here's a clear breakdown of prices and how booking works.

Why choose the helicopter?

The traditional route involves a 16 km trek from Gaurikund. The helicopter cuts that to a short, scenic flight from one of the helipads in the Kedarnath valley — saving a full day and a lot of effort, so you arrive fresh for darshan.

Indicative ticket prices

Fares vary by helipad and season. As a guide, round-trip tickets typically range as below (per person):

  • Phata → Kedarnath (round trip): from ₹4,720
  • Guptkashi → Kedarnath (round trip): from ₹7,750
  • Sersi → Kedarnath (round trip): from ₹4,680

Tip: helicopter slots sell out fast in peak season (May–June). Book early and keep a buffer day for weather delays.
